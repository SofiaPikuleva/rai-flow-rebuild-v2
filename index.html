What this is

We went through rAI’s new first run the way a working driver does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what actually moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at rAI: went through the route demo, your three questions about the working day — with rAI’s voice talking through them — and the trial paywall, read your App Store listing, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you currently show people.

What follows is five ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

Every screen in the prototype is yours — rAI’s face and eyes, the sky gradient, your white answer cards, the route sheet, the map with the purple prompt and your trial-timeline paywall. Only the order and a few lines changed, using things you already ask or own.
