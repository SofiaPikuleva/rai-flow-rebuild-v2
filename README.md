# Growth Audit — rAI

**Live:** https://sofiapikuleva.github.io/rai-flow-rebuild-v2/

Five ranked ideas for rAI AI Assistant for drivers, plus a clickable prototype of the new onboarding and trial paywall rebuilt in rAI's own design. Prepared by Adapty, November 2026, from the live iOS flow and the App Store listing.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Screen | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Name the worry they picked on the paywall | Paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Ask first, then play their day back | Your day, Your route | CR +10–15% |
| 03 | Make the year the plan that saves | Paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 04 | Add an X, and on close offer your USD 63.99 year | Offer on close | ARPU +10–15% |
| 05 | Let them say “Yes” out loud | On the road | CR +5–20% |

Every screen in the prototype is rAI's own design. Only the order and a few lines changed, using things rAI already asks or owns.

## Grounding

Every price and claim comes from rAI's own published material: the US App Store listing (Monthly subscription USD 19.99, Yearly subscription USD 63.99 and USD 119.99) and the in-app screens (the three questions, the route demo, the moment on the road, the trial timeline).

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for rAI.
